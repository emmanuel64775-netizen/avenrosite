What this service includes

Channel optimization

Stream branding

One visual identity across logo, banner, overlays and colors, so you are recognizable on every platform and in every clip.

Promotion and visibility

Real client work

Twitch channel audit for a small horror streamer

A full channel audit report for a streamer who had no viewers finding the channel. Each fix was explained in plain words, and the settings only the owner can change were listed with how to do them.

Channel trailer fixedThe trailer was a 'Starting Soon' video. A real highlight clip was trimmed, published and set as the official trailer, so new visitors see actual content.
Stream title rewrittenThe title did not include 'Horror', one of the main words people search in that game's category. The new title keeps the streamer's humor and is easier to find.
Stream tags correctedTwo tags did not match the game. They were replaced with tags that do, so the recommendation system is not confused.
Content labels addedThe game is mature-rated, so the required content labels were added to keep the channel safe from flags or strikes.
Left for the streamerThe streaming schedule, the offline banner and the pre-roll ads setting. Each one came with the exact steps to do it.
