Twitch channel audit for a small horror streamer

A full channel audit report for a streamer who had no viewers finding the channel. Each fix was explained in plain words, and the settings only the owner can change were listed with how to do them.

Channel trailer fixedThe trailer was a 'Starting Soon' video. A real highlight clip was trimmed, published and set as the official trailer, so new visitors see actual content.
Stream title rewrittenThe title did not include 'Horror', one of the main words people search in that game's category. The new title keeps the streamer's humor and is easier to find.
Stream tags correctedTwo tags did not match the game. They were replaced with tags that do, so the recommendation system is not confused.
Content labels addedThe game is mature-rated, so the required content labels were added to keep the channel safe from flags or strikes.
Left for the streamerThe streaming schedule, the offline banner and the pre-roll ads setting. Each one came with the exact steps to do it.
4fixes made
3items left for the owner

Twitch

Creator Clubs launched in August 2024

Twitch describes Creator Clubs as communities of streamers who make the same type of content. Affiliates and Partners aged 18 or older who stream actively in a club's category can apply. Members get a Twitch-run Discord, events and workshops.

Why it matters: this is the program the Creator Club Plan prepares you for.

Source: Twitch blog
Twitch

Brand sponsorships opened to Affiliates

PocketGamer.biz reports that Twitch opened brand sponsorships to Affiliates in every region. Affiliates must accept the portal terms and complete the sponsorship certification. Affiliates made up over half of the paid streamers in recent Minecraft campaigns.

Why it matters: this is why the Profile Plan starts with the Creator Profile and certification.

Source: PocketGamer.biz
Steam

Steam Next Fest and wishlists

Valve shared 2020 data that Game World Observer reported in 2021: daily wishlist additions rose 421% on average for games in the festival, compared with the two weeks before. Wishlisting stayed about 15% higher for three weeks after.

Why it matters: it shows why preparing a Steam page before a festival is worth the effort. It is Valve's average, not an Avenro result.

Source: Game World Observer
