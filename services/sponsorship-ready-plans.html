Questions

What do I do myself?

You complete the certification course and submit each application from your own account. I guide every step, check the requirements first and write the application text with you.

Which plan should I start with?

Start with the Profile Plan. The certification is what makes your profile eligible for sponsorship campaign invitations through Twitch's system, so it comes first. You can move up to the next plan later.

Do I need to be an Affiliate?

Twitch has opened sponsorships to Affiliates. Requirements change, so I check the current ones for your channel before we start.

How do I pay and when does work start?

Choose a plan, send the short brief, and we agree the details on a call before any work begins.
