What this service includes

Each part, in plain words.

Indie game promotion

General visibility work across the places where players discover new indie titles, from gaming Discords to genre communities.

Steam marketing

Capsule art, trailer pacing, tags and store copy checked against genre benchmarks so your page converts the traffic outreach sends.

Steam wishlist growth

Outreach and promotion aimed at wishlists before launch, since that number drives Steam's launch visibility.

Streamer and influencer outreach

Direct, personal outreach to streamers and creators whose audience overlaps with your genre.

Game PR

Pitching your game to indie press, YouTube previewers and outlets that cover titles like yours, timed to builds and launch week.

Launch promotion

A coordinated push across outreach, PR and community channels, timed to your launch window.

Community growth

Building and running the Discord around your game so players stay, talk and bring friends.

The value to you

Real players and press finding your game before you rely on Steam's algorithm, plus a store page ready to convert the attention.

How it works

Clear steps, with something you can check at each one.

Step 1

Find the right creators

I look for streamers whose past broadcasts and audience fit your game's genre and tone, instead of messaging anyone with a following.

Step 2

Reach out directly

Every message is written for that creator on the platform they use: X, Discord or email. No copy-pasted template.

Step 3

Be upfront about the deal

My role is promotion and outreach. Whether a creator plays or shares your game is their call, and no result is guaranteed.

Step 4

Document everything

Every conversation is logged and screenshotted, so you can see the campaign as it happens.
