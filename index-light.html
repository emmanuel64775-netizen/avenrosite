    How growth actually works

    The slower, honest way, not the fast-looking one.

    Straight talk

    If your channel is not growing, it is rarely one big mistake. Twitch and Kick do not hand out visibility for free, and buying the look of it does not build an audience. Discoverability comes from a channel that is easy to understand at a glance, consistent presentation, and content that gives people a reason to come back.

    • Audits of channel, OBS and profile that fix what costs you real viewers
    • Direct outreach to streamers, communities and press who already reach your audience
    • Branding and positioning so the people who find you stay
    • A growth plan built around your platform and niche
    A word on viewbotting
    • Twitch detects abnormal viewer patterns, and botted channels risk suspension.
    • Bot viewers do not chat, follow or return, so your real engagement ratio drops.
    • Sponsors check for bot activity before a deal.
    • It is a number, not an audience.

    Avenro never uses bots, fake followers or paid non-genuine engagement, on any platform.
